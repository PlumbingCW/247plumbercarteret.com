Plumbing Services in Carteret, NJ

Twenty plumbing services for homes, rentals and small businesses, with a 24/7 emergency line for the urgent ones.

Call (718) 412-8453See all services

Carteret 24/7 Plumbing is a plumber based at 835 Roosevelt Ave in Carteret, NJ, serving the borough and nearby Middlesex County towns. This page lists every service we offer and links to a full guide for each, so you can see what a job involves before you call.

Services marked with an emergency title, such as burst pipe repair and drain cleaning, are answered on the 24/7 line at any hour. Planned work such as repiping, tankless water heater service and basement waterproofing is booked as an appointment, and same-day slots are offered when available.

If you cannot tell which service you need, call and describe what you see, hear or smell. The dispatcher will point you to the right visit, and the guides below explain how each method works and what moves the price. Nothing is priced on this site because the real figure depends on what we find.

Many jobs combine more than one service. A slow main drain may need drain cleaning first and a camera inspection afterward, a failing water heater may call for replacement and a new shutoff valve, and a damp basement can involve a sump pump and a hidden supply leak at once. Tell the dispatcher everything you have noticed, even if it seems unrelated, because a second symptom often points to the real cause.

Every guide on this site is written to fit the National Standard Plumbing Code that New Jersey adopts through the Uniform Construction Code plumbing subcode, N.J.A.C. 5:23-3.15.
