Plumbing Installation in Carteret, NJ

New fixtures, supply lines, drains and appliance hookups installed to code and tested before we leave.

Call (718) 412-8453See all services

Plumbing installation in Carteret, NJ covers new sinks, toilets, tubs, showers, appliance hookups and the supply and drain lines that feed them. It is the work of connecting something new to your water and waste system correctly, with the right shutoffs, venting and slope, and testing it before the job is closed.

Most installs are planned, but our 24/7 line is open when a new fixture leaks on a Saturday night. We explain the scope and a clear price before work starts, check permit rules with the borough when they apply, and finish by running water and showing you where each shutoff is.

What we install

The list is long because a house has many fixtures. Here are the kinds of jobs we do most often.

If your project is not listed, call and describe it. We will tell you honestly whether it is a good fit or whether another trade should lead.

  • Toilets, bidets and bathroom sinks, including vanity swaps.
  • Kitchen sinks, faucets and dishwasher hookups.
  • Showers, tubs and valves, including replacing a worn shower valve.
  • Washing machine boxes, utility sinks and outdoor hose bibs.
  • New supply lines and drains for a bathroom addition or basement bath.
  • Water heater connections, covered in more detail under water heater installation.

Venting, slope and the details that decide whether an install lasts

A drain needs the right pitch so waste moves on its own, and a fixture needs a vent so water does not siphon out of its trap. Get either wrong and the fixture gurgles, drains slowly or lets sewer gas into the room.

This is why new bathroom or kitchen work is more than hooking up a faucet. We trace where the new drain will tie in, check the vent path and plan the supply route before cutting anything. If a vent is clogged on an old house, a symptom like slow drainage may need drain cleaning first.

From first call to a tested fixture

We follow the same path on most installs.

  1. Talk it through.

    Tell us what you plan and send photos of the area, the existing fixtures and the shutoffs.

  2. Site check.

    We measure, check access and discuss the materials, then give a clear price before we begin.

  3. Rough in.

    Supply and drain lines are run, vented and secured, with shutoffs at every fixture.

  4. Set and connect.

    Fixtures are set, connected and sealed, then tested for leaks under normal pressure.

  5. Show and explain.

    We run water, check drains and show you the shutoffs so you know how to isolate each fixture.

Carteret code and permits for new plumbing work

Plumbing work in New Jersey follows the Uniform Construction Code plumbing subcode, which adopts the National Standard Plumbing Code at N.J.A.C. 5:23-3.15. In Carteret, the Building, Construction and Land Use Department at 61 Cooke Avenue reviews plans and inspects permitted work, and its office is open weekdays from 8 a.m. to 4 p.m.

Permit rules vary by job, so ask the borough office or let us confirm for you. Hot water is defined in the code as 120 to 140 degrees Fahrenheit, which is worth knowing when you set a new heater. New Jersey licenses master plumbers through the State Board of Examiners of Master Plumbers, and you can verify any plumber's license through the Division of Consumer Affairs lookup.

What changes the price of an installation

Installation prices depend on the fixture and the house. We give a clear number after we see the space and before we start. The main factors:

  • The fixture itselfA basic toilet and a high-end shower system are different jobs and different materials.
  • Access to existing linesOpen basements are quick. Finished walls and slab floors need more work.
  • New versus replacementA like-for-like swap uses existing connections. New locations need new lines.
  • Venting and drain changesAdding or moving a drain means checking slope and vent routes.
  • Permit and inspectionPermitted jobs add inspection steps and some scheduling.

Homeowners insurance does not cover upgrades or planned installs. Some policies cover damage if a new connection fails later. For your real price, call (718) 412-8453.

Landlords, tenants and shared buildings

With roughly 45 percent of Carteret homes rented, many installs are done for owners or managers while a tenant is living there. We schedule around the household, keep the work area contained and give the owner a written record.

For a multi-unit house, we also check which shutoffs serve which unit, so replacing one fixture does not take the whole building offline. If an old line fails during the install, our crews can switch to repiping for the damaged section.

When a new fixture leaks at night

A fresh install that leaks needs attention the same day. Close the fixture's shutoff valve, or the main if there is none, and call the 24/7 line. We take calls nights and weekends and book same-day appointments when available.

Plumbing Installation questions from Carteret customers

Do I need a permit for a plumbing install in Carteret?

It depends on the job. A simple like-for-like swap may not, but new lines or a bathroom addition usually do. Ask the Carteret construction office at 61 Cooke Avenue or let us confirm for you.

Can you install a fixture I bought myself?

Usually yes. We check that it fits and that the connections are right before we start. Note that if a part is missing or incompatible, we will tell you before work begins.

Do you install appliances like dishwashers and washers?

We install the plumbing connections: supply lines, shutoff valves and drains. Electrical hookups and heavy appliance delivery are handled separately.

How do I know the plumber is licensed?

New Jersey licenses master plumbers through the State Board of Examiners of Master Plumbers. You can check any plumber through the Division of Consumer Affairs license lookup.

What if the new fixture leaks after you leave?

Call the 24/7 line. We return to check connections, and we say plainly if the leak is from our work or from an older line.

Do you install in Avenel and Port Reading?

Yes. We serve Avenel, Port Reading, Fords, Woodbridge Township and Perth Amboy from Carteret.
