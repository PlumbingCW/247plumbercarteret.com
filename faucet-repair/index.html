Emergency Faucet and Sink Repair in Carteret, NJ

Dripping faucets, leaking sink traps, low pressure and burst supply lines repaired, with a 24/7 line for active leaks.

Call (718) 412-8453See all services

Faucet and sink repair in Carteret, NJ means stopping a drip, sealing a leak under the basin or restoring flow to a fixture that has gone weak. If water is spraying or pooling in the cabinet right now, shut the small valves under the sink and call our 24/7 emergency line.

Many sink problems look dramatic and cost very little to fix. A cartridge, a washer, a trap nut or a clogged aerator accounts for most of what we see. We find the exact cause, explain it and fix it on the visit when the part is on the truck.

Sink leaking under the cabinet: what to do first

A slow cabinet leak ruins the base board and breeds mold, while a burst supply line can empty a lot of water in minutes. Treat any active leak as urgent, and use the steps below even if it is the middle of the night.

  • Turn both small valves under the sink clockwise. Hot is on the left and cold is on the right in most kitchens.
  • If a valve will not turn or keeps dripping, close the main shutoff where the water enters the house.
  • Empty the cabinet and set a bucket under the trap, then dry the base with towels.
  • Photograph the wet area for your insurer or landlord, then call the line so we can arrive with the right fittings.

Finding the leak: faucet, supply line, trap or drain

Leaks under a sink come from four places, and a dry paper towel will find the right one. Run the water, then wipe each joint and watch which one gets wet first.

Wet only while the faucet runs usually means the faucet body or supply lines. Wet only after the basin drains points to the trap or the drain tailpiece. A constant wet spot at the wall suggests the shutoff valve or the pipe behind it.

  • Drip from the spout when off: worn cartridge, washer or seat.
  • Handle leaking at its base: failed O-ring or cartridge seal.
  • Water under the basin only during use: loose or cracked supply hose.
  • Water under the basin only during draining: trap nut, gasket or tailpiece.
  • Wet at the wall: stop valve packing or a joint inside the wall.

Fix the faucet or replace it?

A faucet that drips is not always worth saving. This table is how we decide, and we show you the numbers on the part first.

Low pressure and sputtering taps after water main work

Middlesex Water's RENEW 2023 project replaced about 26,000 feet of main across Carteret and part of Port Reading, including streets such as Casey Street, Marion Street, Mulberry Street and Sycamore Street. Crews also relocated some customer meters to outside pits.

After that kind of work, small pieces of sediment can loosen inside household pipes and lodge in faucet screens. If a tap suddenly sputters or runs slow, unscrew the aerator at the spout and rinse it. If the problem stays, the cartridge may be clogged and we can swap it. Mineral scale is a second cause, since Middlesex Water's report shows hardness ranging from 12 to 314 parts per million across its system.

Sources: Middlesex Water Company: $11.1 million RENEW project in Carteret and Port Reading. Middlesex Water Company annual water quality report.

How we repair a faucet

The sequence below is the same for kitchen sinks, bathroom basins and utility sinks.

  1. Shut off and strip.

    We close the stop valves, relieve pressure and remove the handle to reach the cartridge.

  2. Inspect the seat.

    Worn seats, scale and cracked O-rings are shown to you, not just replaced.

  3. Repair or replace.

    The cartridge is swapped, or the faucet is replaced if the body has failed.

  4. Check the lines.

    Supply hoses, stop valves and the trap are tested while the water runs.

What decides faucet and sink repair cost

We do not quote a flat figure for a faucet we have not seen, because a cartridge swap and a replaced stop valve are different jobs. A clear price is given before work starts, and these are the main factors:

  • The partStandard cartridges and washers are inexpensive. Specialty or imported fixtures can need special orders.
  • Fixture conditionCorroded stop valves under the sink often need replacing to finish the repair.
  • AccessA tight cabinet or a wall-mounted faucet takes longer than an exposed deck-mounted one.
  • Number of fixturesTwo or three taps in the same bathroom are done more efficiently on one visit.
  • Time of callOvernight and weekend leaks are emergency visits and are priced as such, with the number stated first.

Homeowners insurance generally covers sudden leaks but not slow seepage over time. Ask your insurer. For your real number, call (718) 412-8453.

Sinks that will not drain are a different job

A basin that fills and drains slowly is a blockage, not a faucet problem. Hair and soap in a bathroom trap or grease in a kitchen trap is the usual cause, and a plunger or a trap cleaning often solves it.

If both the kitchen sink and the dishwasher back up together, the clog is past the trap. That is drain cleaning work, and when the kitchen has a grinder attached the issue may be the garbage disposal instead. We check both before charging for the wrong repair.

Frozen, noisy or hammering taps

Knocking in the pipes when a tap shuts is water hammer, caused by sudden stops in flow. It can be cured with an arrestor or by securing loose pipe. Freezing is a winter problem: if a tap in an unheated space stops flowing in a hard freeze, leave the tap slightly open, warm the pipe gently and call us if it splits.

A split pipe is a burst pipe repair and should be treated as an active leak. Shut the water at the main first.

Code and safety references for faucet and sink repair

Faucet repairs are usually simple part replacements, and any rerouting of supply lines falls under the plumbing subcode, N.J.A.C. 5:23-3.15, which adopts the National Standard Plumbing Code. After water main work, loosened sediment can clog aerators, so check them first when flow drops.

Faucet and Sink Repair questions from Carteret customers

Do you handle faucet and sink repair emergencies at night or on weekends in Carteret?

Yes. The 24/7 line is answered nights, weekends and holidays, and we book same-day appointments when we can. Tell us whether you have closed the valves under the sink so we know how urgent the leak is.

Why does my faucet drip after I turn it off?

A worn washer, seat or cartridge is the usual cause, and the fix is often a single inexpensive part. Dripping can also come from high household pressure, which we can test with a gauge.

Is it worth repairing an old faucet?

Often yes, if the model is common and the body is not corroded. If parts are discontinued or the faucet needs a second repair, replacement is usually the better use of money.

My water pressure dropped at only one sink. What is wrong?

A clogged aerator or cartridge is most likely. If all taps are weak, the problem is upstream at a valve or in the service line, which is where our water service line repair team can test.

Can you fix a leaking kitchen sink drain?

Yes. Trap nuts, gaskets and tailpieces are replaced regularly, and we test the full run with water before we leave.

Do you cover Fords and Woodbridge for faucet repair?

Yes. Fords and Woodbridge Township are dispatched from our Carteret office, including after hours.
