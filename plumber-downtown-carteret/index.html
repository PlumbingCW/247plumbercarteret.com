Plumber in Downtown Carteret, NJ

Plumbing help for the Washington Avenue arts and business district, where new apartments sit above shops and restaurants.

Call (718) 412-8453See all services

Downtown Carteret is the borough's Downtown Cultural Arts & Business District, centered on Washington Avenue and the Carteret Performing Arts & Events Center. It is the most active redevelopment area in the borough. New apartment buildings with ground floor retail have been built or approved along Washington Avenue.

That makes this page different from a guide to a block of older houses. The plumbing questions here come from managed apartment buildings, small shops and restaurants. The first call is often to a building manager, so we explain who handles what.

Downtown Carteret at a glance

Where it sits.

Along Washington Avenue, across from the Carteret Performing Arts & Events Center, which opened in 2021.

21 Washington.

A 153 apartment project with studios, one and two bedrooms, a two story parking garage and first floor retail.

29 Washington.

A building directly across the avenue with 64 units and 12,500 square feet of retail.

Plumbing focus.

Shared stacks, building water heaters, restaurant drains and the owner and tenant questions that come with them.

Before you call about a problem downtown

  1. Tell building management.

    In an apartment building, report the problem to the manager first. They control access to risers, mechanical rooms and shared equipment.

  2. Find your unit shutoff.

    Close the valve under the sink, behind the toilet or at the unit's main if you can reach it. Leave building valves to staff.

  3. Protect the shop floor.

    In a shop or restaurant, shut off the equipment that is feeding water, move stock off the floor and keep customers clear.

  4. Take photos and call.

    Photograph the damage for the owner and your records, then call (718) 412-8453.

Who handles what in a managed building

In a managed building the owner usually hires the plumber and controls access to the risers and mechanical rooms. A tenant who calls a plumber directly may be told the owner has to approve the visit. Report the problem first, then ask the manager which contractor they use or whether you may arrange the work yourself.

For the owner or manager, a plumber who can explain the cause to the tenant and the owner in the same terms saves arguments later. That is how we handle multi-unit calls. We trace which units share the line before we propose a fix.

Verified facts about the district

These points come from the borough's own announcement and from Wikipedia. Details such as completion dates change, so check the sources for the latest.

  • The borough announced 21 Washington as a $41 million project by Kalian Companies and Epstein Family Investments, with 153 apartments, a two story garage and first floor retail.
  • Kalian Companies also operates 29 Washington, directly across the avenue, with 64 units and 12,500 square feet of retail.
  • The borough describes a $300 million plan that includes mixed-use buildings and more than 30,000 square feet of commercial space.
  • The 21 Washington project was expected to finish in early 2025 when it was announced.
  • The Carteret Performing Arts Center opened in 2021, according to Wikipedia.

Sources: Borough of Carteret: 21 Washington. Wikipedia: Carteret, New Jersey.

Restaurants and shops on Washington Avenue

Kitchens send grease, food and hot water into the drain, and a line that serves a kitchen needs regular attention. Whether a grease interceptor is required, and how it must be maintained, is set by local rules. Ask the borough construction office at 61 Cooke Avenue and the sewer provider before you install or change one.

Commercial buildings can also be required to protect the public water supply from backflow under the Safe Drinking Water Act framework. If your supplier or the borough sends a notice about a backflow device or test, call us and we will go over what it asks. Our commercial plumbing page lists the work.

If water is actively leaking

An active leak in a building can reach the units below within minutes. Act in this order.

  • Close the shutoff for your unit or fixture and tell building management right away.
  • Move belongings off the floor and keep clear of outlets and lights near the water.
  • Photograph the source and the damage, then call (718) 412-8453.

Other parts of Carteret

Downtown sits between older residential sections. Chrome to the southeast has many small apartment buildings, and West Carteret is mostly houses. For a leak that is hard to find in either, see leak detection.

Downtown Carteret plumbing questions

Is Downtown Carteret a residential neighborhood?

It is mixed. The borough calls it the Downtown Cultural Arts & Business District, and it includes new apartment buildings, shops and restaurants along Washington Avenue.

I rent in a new building. Can I hire a plumber myself?

Usually the owner decides. Check your lease and report the problem to the manager first, because the owner controls the building systems and often pays for repairs.

Do restaurants downtown need special drain equipment?

Rules vary. Ask the borough construction office at 61 Cooke Avenue and the sewer provider before installing or altering any grease interceptor.

Where does the district sit?

Along Washington Avenue, across from the Carteret Performing Arts & Events Center. The borough has not published a single boundary map that we found, so call with your address if unsure.

Do you work in apartment buildings and shops?

Multi-unit and commercial work is part of what this site lists. Call, describe the building and what is wrong, and ask whether we can take the job.
