WHO WE ARE

About Carteret 24/7 Plumbing

A Carteret-based plumber with a phone line that is answered around the clock.

Call (718) 412-8453See all services

A plumber based in Carteret, not dispatched from far away

Carteret 24/7 Plumbing works from 835 Roosevelt Ave, Carteret, NJ 07008, on the borough's main commercial street. That address matters because our trucks start from the same borough where many of our customers live, and because we know the conditions that make Carteret plumbing different. Roosevelt Avenue itself runs through West Carteret, the part of town that spent 25 years under a state sewer ban.

We also work in the towns around Carteret: Port Reading, Avenel, Woodbridge Township, Fords and Perth Amboy. Each has its own plumbing story, from the tidal Arthur Kill to higher ground in Fords, and each has a page that explains it.

What we do

We handle the plumbing problems homeowners, landlords and small businesses call about most. Drain and sewer work includes drain cleaning, hydro jetting and sewer camera inspection. We repair and replace tank and tankless water heaters, find hidden leaks with leak detection, and repair burst pipes and failed water service lines.

We also fix toilets, faucets and disposals, service sump pumps, install new fixtures and run repiping projects. The full list is on the services page. If a job is outside what we do, we say so on the phone instead of sending a truck that cannot finish it.

How we work

The phone line is answered 24 hours a day, every day, because plumbing failures do not wait for business hours. A dispatcher asks what you see, tells you what to shut off and books the visit. We offer same-day appointments when the schedule allows.

Before any work starts you get a clear price. We do not publish prices on this site, because a clogged sink and a collapsed main line behind a slab are different jobs, and a number that ignores that does not help you. We also explain what we found in plain words, so you can decide with the facts.

We are licensed and insured. New Jersey licenses master plumbers through the State Board of Examiners of Master Plumbers, part of the Division of Consumer Affairs, and you can verify any plumber through the Division's online license lookup. We encourage you to check anyone you hire, including us.

How we handle rentals and shared buildings

About 45 percent of Carteret homes are rented, and many of our calls involve both a tenant and an owner. We give both the same account of the cause and the repair, which keeps arguments about who pays short. For multi-unit buildings we check shared stacks and main lines first, since clearing one apartment's trap rarely fixes a shared problem.

Local conditions we plan around

Plumbing problems follow geography. Carteret borders the tidal Arthur Kill and the Rahway River, and Hurricane Sandy in October 2012 sent storm surge up to 10 feet deep across the waterfront. That history is why we ask about floor drains and sump pumps early on a wet-weather call, and why our sump pump repair page spends time on check valves and discharge lines.

Water quality and service lines matter too. Middlesex Water Company supplies Carteret, and its annual water quality report shows hardness that varies a lot across its system, so we tell customers to read their own report instead of trusting a single number. When a repair touches a service line, we explain whether the pipe is yours to maintain or the utility's, since the answer changes who pays.

On a typical call we also think about the age of the house. Older homes in Carteret and the neighboring towns often have mixed pipe materials, so a repair in one spot can reveal a worn section next to it. We point that out when we see it and leave the decision to you.

What a good plumbing visit looks like

A good visit starts with a question about what you saw before the problem began. We then look at the fixture, test the line and tell you what is wrong before we pick up a tool. You hear the price, the options and the likely outcome, and you decide.

When the job is done we run the water, check nearby fixtures and tell you what to watch for in the next few days. If the real fix is bigger than the call, such as a failing sewer line, we say so plainly and explain how to plan for it.

Why we built this site

Carteret has few plumbing companies with a local address and a page that explains local conditions. We wanted a site where a homeowner at midnight can find the number, learn what to shut off and see what the job involves. Every service page explains the method, the warning signs and the factors that move the price, and our plumbing blog goes into longer how-to topics.

If something on this site is wrong or out of date, tell us. Call our line or write to contact@247plumbercarteret.com and we will fix it.

Every guide on this site is written to fit the National Standard Plumbing Code that New Jersey adopts through the Uniform Construction Code plumbing subcode, N.J.A.C. 5:23-3.15.
