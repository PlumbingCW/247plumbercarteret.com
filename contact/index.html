What to tell the dispatcher

A few details help us send the right person with the right parts. You do not need technical words, just a clear description of what you see, hear or smell. The more specific you are, the more likely the first visit finishes the job.

  • Where in the house the problem is: kitchen, bathroom, basement, laundry, yard or the street side of the property.
  • What you can see: water on the floor, a slow drain, a gurgling toilet, a cold shower, a wet ceiling or a sewage smell.
  • What you have already done: closed the main valve, switched off the breaker, stopped using fixtures or tried a plunger.
  • Your address and ZIP code, and whether you rent or own, so we can reach the landlord if needed.
  • Whether anyone is in danger. If you smell gas, leave first and follow PSE&G's published guidelines: move at least 350 feet away, then call 1-800-880-PSEG (7734) or 911.

Why we do not use a contact form

A form does not help when a pipe is spraying water, and it adds a wait that a phone call does not. Every request on this site goes to the phone line. If your question is not urgent, email contact@247plumbercarteret.com and we will reply during normal business hours.

When you call, you may be asked for your address, the best number to reach you and a short description of the problem. We state the price before work begins, and nothing starts without your agreement. Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes.
